Case Studies
------------

### Case Study 1: University Scholarship Allocation

Evaluates scholarship applicants based on aggregate marks, subject-wise minimum marks, attendance, and family income. Identifies eligible students, ranks applicants, and resolves ties using predefined criteria.

### Case Study 2: Hospital Billing and Patient Classification

Calculates hospital bills using consultation, diagnostic, and treatment charges. Applies concessions, validates billing information, and classifies bills according to payment categories.

### Case Study 9: Multi-Currency Travel Budget Analysis

Converts travel expenses into Indian Rupees using supplied exchange rates. Calculates conversion fees, compares spending against a budget, and identifies the largest expense category.

### Case Study 10: Campus Event Financial Planning

Calculates venue, equipment, catering, transport, and certificate expenses. Determines participant contributions, sponsorship shortfalls, and the minimum number of registrations required to break even.

### Case Study 11: Examination Result and Grace-Mark Validation

Processes marks across multiple subjects, applies subject-wise passing criteria and grace-mark rules, validates maximum marks, and generates final examination classifications.

### Case Study 12: Attendance Recovery Computation

Calculates current attendance percentages and determines the number of additional classes required to reach a specified attendance threshold under different future attendance scenarios.

### Case Study 13: ATM Transaction Sequence Simulation

Simulates deposits and withdrawals, maintains the account balance, enforces withdrawal limits, rejects invalid transactions, and generates a transaction summary.

### Case Study 14: Warehouse Inventory Reconciliation

Compares opening stock, incoming quantities, dispatched quantities, and recorded closing stock. Detects discrepancies and calculates the quantity required to replenish inventory.

### Case Study 15: Smart Parking Fee Management

Calculates parking charges based on vehicle categories, entry and exit times, hourly rates, daily limits, overnight parking, and penalty conditions.

### Case Study 16: Multi-Day Water Tank Simulation

Simulates water levels over multiple intervals by processing inflow and outflow quantities. Detects overflow and shortages and identifies the first interval when the target water level is reached.
